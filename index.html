DUBURI NAVID

"Dive Deep. Discover More."

Controls

W / ↑ - Swim Up

S / ↓ - Swim Down

A / ← - Move Left

D / → - Move Right

ESC - Pause

How to Play

Objective: Explore the deep ocean, collect treasures, and survive!

Collectibles:

  • 🪙 Coin - +10 points
  • 💎 Treasure - +100 points
  • 🏺 Rare Artifact - +250 points
  • 👑 Bonus Treasure - +500 points
  • 💨 Oxygen Tank - Restores oxygen

Dangers:

  • 🦈 Shark - Chases you when nearby
  • 🪼 Jellyfish - Floats vertically
  • 💣 Mine - Explodes on contact

Tips:

  • Watch your oxygen level!
  • Collect oxygen tanks to survive longer
  • Go deeper to discover new zones
  • Avoid enemies to maintain health
